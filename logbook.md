# Observation & Process Logbook

Here we document our field tests, calibration steps, RFI (Radio Frequency Interference) measurements, and initial spectrum observations:

* **[November 2026] - Initial Setup & Design:** Finalized hardware components (120 cm dish, cantenna feedhorn, Nooelec SAWbird+ H1 LNA, and RTL-SDR V3) and created the project repository.
* **[Upcoming] - Feedhorn & LNA Assembly:** Mechanical alignment of the feedhorn at the focal point of the dish.
* **[Upcoming] - Baseline & RFI Tests:** Noise floor measurements and 50 Ohm termination calibration.
* **[Upcoming] - First Hydrogen Spectrum:** Pointing towards the Milky Way galactic plane to capture the 1420 MHz neutral hydrogen line.
